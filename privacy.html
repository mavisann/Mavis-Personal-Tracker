Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Mavis (StudyBoard) handles information when you use this educational productivity application. Mavis is maintained by Prince Semillano.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we process your username, optional email address, and an encrypted password or Google sign-in identifier. When you use the dashboard, we store the courses, tasks, schedules, budgets, and settings you choose to enter.

How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate you, synchronize your personal workspace, provide dashboard features, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information or use private study data for advertising.

Service providers

The static frontend is hosted by GitHub Pages. The API is hosted by Render and uses PostgreSQL. Authentication and optional Google sign-in may use Supabase and Google identity services as configured by the deployment. These providers process data under their own terms and security controls.

Retention and security

Account and workspace data is retained while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. We use HTTPS, signed authentication tokens, and server-side password hashing. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so do not store highly sensitive information in your study notes or budget entries.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your account data. Contact prince.semillanoquilantang@gmail.com and include the username associated with your request. You can also open an issue on GitHub.

Children and changes

Mavis is intended for students and is not knowingly directed to children under 13. We may update this policy as the service changes; the date above identifies the current version.
